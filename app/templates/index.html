How it works

Start with judicial opinions to see the workflow, then add appellate oral argument transcripts or firm-supplied documents as additional sources. Firm documents are processed locally, anonymized before analysis, and never used as raw identifying text. Later, add `.txt` or PDF files to data/opinions/ or data/transcripts/, or upload one-off files in either format. PDFs are extracted to text automatically.
